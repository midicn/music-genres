## 这一站是做什么的

流派与风格站按**听感**给音乐分类。它回答三个问题：
**这是什么味道、从哪里来、想听这一类该从哪首开始。**

## 会收录哪些内容

- **大类** —— 古典与浪漫、爵士、摇滚与流行、电子、世界音乐。
- **子风格** —— 大类之下的分支，讲清它和母类差在哪里：节奏、音色、编制、场合。
- **谱系图** —— 谁受了谁的影响、什么时候分岔。这是本站的核心可视化。
- **代表曲目** —— 每一个风格都给出库里真实存在的例子，可以边读边听。
- **中国乐种** —— 不在本站，全部归中国音乐站；这里只保留一条通往它那里的入口。

## 每条长什么样

- 开头是**听感描述**：不写「它很抒情」，而是写「你能听见什么」——
  用什么节奏型、什么音色、什么演奏方式、什么曲式。
- 然后是**来历**：从哪种音乐里长出来，在什么场合流行起来。
- 最后是**边界**：和最容易混的那个风格差在哪。

## 怎么读

1. **从大类进** —— 先弄清几个大类的差别，再往下看子风格。
2. **看谱系图** —— 一张地图比十条条目更快说明「谁是谁的上一代」。
3. **横向比** —— 同一段旋律在不同风格里的处理差别最直观。

## 现在到哪一步

条目正在陆续上线。会先立起**大类的骨架**，再逐个填子风格 ——
这样任何一条上线时，都能立刻被放进已有的地图里，而不是孤零零地挂着。

## What this station is for

This station classifies music **by how it sounds**. It answers three questions:
**what does it feel like, where does it come from, and what should I start with if I want
to hear more of it.**

## What it will contain

- **Broad categories** — classical and Romantic, jazz, rock and pop, electronic, world music.
- **Sub-styles** — the branches under each, explaining exactly how they differ from the
  parent: rhythm, timbre, line-up, setting.
- **A family tree** — who influenced whom, and when the branches split. This is the
  station's main visualisation.
- **Representative tracks** — every style gets examples that really exist in the library,
  so you can listen while you read.
- **Chinese traditions** — not here; they all live on the Chinese music station. This
  station keeps only a single doorway to it.

## What each entry looks like

- It opens with **how it sounds** — not "it is lyrical" but what you actually hear: which
  rhythmic patterns, which timbres, which ways of playing, which forms.
- Then **where it came from**: which music it grew out of, and in what setting it became
  popular.
- Finally **the boundary**: how it differs from the style it is most often confused with.

## How to read it

1. **Start from the broad categories** — get the main divisions clear, then go down.
2. **Read the family tree** — one map explains "who came from whom" faster than ten entries.
3. **Compare across** — the same melody handled in different styles is the clearest test.

## Where it stands today

Entries are being added over time, starting with **the skeleton of the broad categories**
and then filling in sub-styles one by one — so that every entry, when it arrives, already
has a place on the map instead of hanging on its own.
